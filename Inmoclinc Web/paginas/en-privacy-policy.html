The legal basis for processing personal data may include:

  • Legitimate business interests where applicable.

Personal data will be retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected and, thereafter, for the periods required by applicable legislation.

  • Request deletion of their data.
  • Object to processing.
  • Restrict processing.
  • Request data portability.

Requests may be sent to:

info@inmoclinc.com
