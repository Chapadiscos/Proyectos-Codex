COOKIE POLICY

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device when visiting a website. They help websites remember information about browsing activity and user preferences.

Types of Cookies Used

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the website and do not require user consent.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to analyse website traffic and improve the performance and content of the website.

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember user preferences and settings to enhance the browsing experience.

Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be used to provide relevant advertising based on users' browsing behaviour.

Cookie Management

Upon first accessing the website, users may:

  • Accept all cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Configure their cookie preferences.

Users may modify their consent preferences at any time through the "Cookie Settings" link available on the website.

Disabling Cookies

Users may configure their browser settings to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.
